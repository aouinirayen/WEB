About Us

Who are we? 🚌

Based in Tunisia, TecnoTuners is our team name. We are a group of passionate students from different governorates all over the country united by a common goal: making transport better.

What we do?

We raise the bar in the transportation sector through our innovative solution TransEasier — a platform, mobile app and a network of connected panels that redefine transportation in a more efficient and convenient way.
